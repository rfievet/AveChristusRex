## Verified key facts
- Russell began publishing *Zion's Watch Tower* in **July 1879**; society formed **1881**, incorporated in Pennsylvania **December 15, 1884**. (verified)
- Name **"Jehovah's witnesses" adopted July 26, 1931** at a Columbus, Ohio convention under J. F. Rutherford, based on Isaiah 43:10. (verified)
- Failed/revised dates **1914, 1925, 1975**; *Millions Now Living Will Never Die* booklet published **1920**, predicting **1925**. (verified)
- NWT uses **"Jehovah" 237 times in the NT** where the Greek reads *kyrios*/*theos*; **no extant critical Greek NT manuscript contains the Tetragrammaton**. (verified — 237 is the Watch Tower's own figure, corroborated in secondary literature)
- John 1:1 "a god" criticized by **Metzger (1953), Barclay (1953), Mantey (1980)**; Colossians 1:15-17 flagged by Metzger. (verified)
- **Council of Nicaea A.D. 325** convened by Constantine; Arianism = Son created, not co-eternal. (verified)
- Trinity rejected; cross used until **1931**, rejected from **1936**; 144,000 from Rev 7:4 / 14:1-4; "new light" from Prov 4:18; Governing Body formally established **1971**; "faithful and discreet slave" self-identified with the Governing Body since **2012**. (verified)

2. **Blood-transfusion ban year (1945)** — the ban is commonly dated to a 1945 *Watchtower* article, but I could not re-verify the exact article/issue in this environment. Confirm before asserting the specific year. (`jw-doc-blood`)
3. **Jesus = Michael the archangel — exact citation** — this is consistent, well-known JW teaching, but I could not verify the specific *Watchtower* issue/page that most explicitly states it. Cite a specific issue only after confirming it. (`jw-doc-michael`)
4. **Arius as "founder" of Arianism** — historians (e.g., Rowan Williams) note Arius was not literally the sole originator of "Arianism." The Christological *content* parallel to JW doctrine is sound; avoid overstating Arius's personal role. (`jw-doc-arianism`)
5. **"Prophet" framing (Deut 18)** — modern JW literature denies ever claiming to be an inspired prophet. The debate point stands (dates set in God's name failed), but phrase it as the organization having made failed predictions, not as it formally self-designating "inspired prophet."
